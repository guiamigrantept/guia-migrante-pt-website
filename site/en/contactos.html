Urgent situations

Useful numbers in Portugal

Do not use emergency lines for routine immigration questions.

112 — Emergency

Sudden illness, accident, fire or immediate danger. Free and available 24 hours.

Call 112

808 24 24 24 — SNS 24

Health triage, advice and referral. Clinical contact available 24 hours.

Call SNS 24

144 — Social Emergency

Urgent situations of social vulnerability and lack of protection. Free, 24 hours.

Call 144

300 502 502 — Social Security

General information on Social Security services. Weekdays, 09:00–18:00.

Call Social Security
