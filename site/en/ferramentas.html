    Immigration glossary in plain English

    AIMA

    Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum.

    NIF

    Portuguese Tax Identification Number.

    NISS

    Portuguese Social Security Identification Number.

    SNS / NHS user number

    Identifier used in the Portuguese public health system.

    CLAIM

    Local Migrant Integration Support Centre.

    IRN

    Institute of Registries and Notaries, involved in nationality matters.

    CPLP

    Community of Portuguese Language Countries.

    Expression of Interest

    Former mechanism linked to Articles 88(2) and 89(2), revoked for new applications in 2024; older and transitional cases have their own treatment.

    Family reunification

    Procedure for eligible family members when the statutory conditions are met.
