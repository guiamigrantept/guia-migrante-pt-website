What changed recently?

A concise log of recent changes that may affect residence, nationality, identification numbers or essential services.

Checked 20/08/2026Official sources
Editorial review — 20/08/2026: key information on NIF, NISS, NHS/SNS user numbers, the combined identification service, AIMA, renewals, transitional cases, fees, contacts and nationality law was rechecked.
20 AUG 2026 · RENEWALS

Renewal Portal window currently runs to 31/10/2026

AIMA indicates the portal for residence permits expired or expiring from 1 July 2025 to 31 October 2026, with additional current functions for ARI and certain permanent EU residence documents.

Read more →
20 AUG 2026 · AIMA

Missing documents can be added online in certain first-grant cases

For some residence-permit applications initiated directly with AIMA, the Contact Form may be used to add missing documents. AIMA excludes Renewal Portal and services.aima.gov.pt cases from this specific mechanism.

Read more →
20 AUG 2026 · FEES

AIMA fee table in force since 01/03/2026

The amount depends on the procedure. Where eligible, AIMA indicates a 25% digital/online reduction and 10% assisted-digital reduction compared with the corresponding in-person amount.

Read more →
15 JUN 2026 · IDENTIFIERS

NIF + NISS + NHS/SNS user number in one request

gov.pt currently lists 12 participating Citizen Shops for eligible foreign nationals with an address in Portugal.

Read more →
19 MAY 2026 · NATIONALITY

New Nationality Law rules entered into force

For new general adult naturalisation applications by legal residence: seven years for Portuguese-speaking-country nationals and EU citizens, and ten years for nationals of other States, subject to the remaining statutory requirements. Pending cases on 19 May keep the previous wording.

20 AUG 2026 · NIF

Official NIF request remains free

Portuguese and foreign individuals, resident or non-resident, can request NIF. Documentation and tax representation depend on the situation.

NIF guide →
20 AUG 2026 · NISS / HEALTH

Online NISS and NHS/SNS user-number information confirmed

Foreign nationals can request NISS online and the official request is free. The NHS/SNS user number can be assigned at a first visit to a public health unit; the number alone does not guarantee full cost coverage.
