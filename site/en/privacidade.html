Privacy · updated 24/08/2026

Clear privacy, without invasive tracking.

Guia Migrante PT works without a personal account. This policy explains what is stored when you browse the portal, send a message or arrive through a campaign.

No advertising cookiesLimited first-party analyticsMessages: up to 180 daysAnalytics: 90 days
Simple summary

We collect only what is needed to operate and improve.

You do not need an account, we do not sell data to advertisers and we do not use persistent identifiers to follow people across pages or services.

The Guide's analytics database does not store your IP address, user-agent, cookies, user ID or the full URL you came from.
Essential rule

Do not send sensitive documents.

  • Passport, residence card or permit.
  • Tax numbers, case numbers or personal evidence.
  • Passwords, PINs, authentication codes or digital identity credentials.
  • Banking or medical documentation.
1. “Contact the Guide” channel

Data processed when you send a message

The form allows an optional name and email, together with the selected subject and message. The language, source page inside the portal, date/time and internal message status are also stored for administration.

Name and emailOptional. Email is used only if you want a reply.
MessageStored for administration review and management.
Abuse preventionA technical hash of email/subject/message is created to detect recent duplicates.

The Guide does not store the IP address or user-agent in its message database. Message content is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Retention: messages and the related technical hash are deleted after a maximum of 180 days.
2. Cookie-free analytics

Measurement to improve the site and evaluate campaigns

The Guide uses its own limited analytics system to understand useful pages, language usage, completed contact submissions and campaign effectiveness.

We recordEvent type, page path, language and date/time.
CampaignsUTM source, medium and campaign when present.
External originOnly the hostname when supplied by the browser, never the full URL.

To attribute a contact submission later in the same browsing session to the campaign that brought the visit, only the campaign's UTM labels may be kept temporarily in browser sessionStorage until that session ends. No user or device identifier is created.

We may also count clicks to official sources, storing only the destination hostname. Advertising cookies, persistent IDs, IP addresses, user-agent strings and contact-form content are not stored in analytics.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track are respected: when your browser signals either preference, the Guide's JavaScript does not send analytics events or save campaign labels for measurement.

Retention: analytics events are deleted after 90 days and administrative reports show aggregated data.

3. Hosting technical data

Cloudflare and site delivery

Like any website, the hosting and network provider may process technical information required to deliver pages, mitigate attacks and keep the service available, including IP address, request time and connection characteristics.

This infrastructure processing is separate from the Guide's own analytics database, where IP addresses are not stored.

4. Storage on your device

Preferences and PWA

Some features use local or session browser storage to remember checklists, visual preferences, dates or, where applicable, the campaign labels described above. The installable version may also cache public files for better operation on weak connections.

These mechanisms do not create a Guide account and can be cleared from your browser's site data.

5. Cookies

No advertising cookies

The Guide does not use advertising cookies or cookies intended to build behavioural profiles. The measurement system described above works without cookies and persistent identifiers.

6. External links

When you leave the Guide

When you open AIMA, gov.pt, Justice, Social Security, SNS or another external website, that service's privacy policy applies. The Guide does not control processing performed by those websites.

7. Purposes and rights

Why we use the data and how to contact us

Contact-form data is used to receive, manage and, when you provide an email address, reply to the communication you initiated. Limited analytics data is used for operational security, portal improvement and aggregate assessment of content and campaign effectiveness.

Access and correction. You may ask for information about data linked to a message you sent and request correction where applicable.
Deletion and restriction. You may request deletion or restriction where applicable and technically identifiable.
Objection. You may object to processing where that right applies to the specific case.
Contact. Use the “Contact the Guide” channel and state that your request concerns privacy.

Because name and email are optional, an anonymous message may not later be technically linkable to a specific person.

Go to the contact channel →

8. Changes to this policy

We update before increasing data collection

If new services, providers, behavioural advertising, cookies or other relevant processing are added, this policy must be reviewed before that use. The review date appears at the top of this page.
